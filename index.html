🎨 Design Retrospective
View Full →

Complete redesign of the Life Dashboard using world-class UX/UI principles. Key learnings from the process:

1. Design Systems Matter
Starting with a design system (colors, spacing, typography) creates consistency and speed.
2. Background Creates Atmosphere
Animated gradient mesh transforms feel from "webpage" to "experience."
3. Glass-Morphism is Powerful
Backdrop blur with semi-transparent backgrounds creates depth and modernity.
4. Animation Should Guide
Every animation needs purpose. Subtle > Flashy. Under 300ms.
5. Bento Grids Organize
Modern, scannable, visually interesting information layout.
6. Warm Colors Feel Personal
Orange/amber feels creative and energetic vs corporate blue.
"Good design is invisible. Users shouldn't notice the design - they should effortlessly achieve their goals while feeling delighted by small moments of craft."
